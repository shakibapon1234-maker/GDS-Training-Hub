Amadeus Help & Shortcuts
Basic Commands:
• AN[Date][Origin][Dest] (e.g. AN15NOVDACBKK)
• SS1Y1 (Sell flight seat from line 1)
• NM1[Surname]/[Name] [Title]
• AP [Phone]
• TKTL / TKOK
• RF [Sign] ; ER (Save PNR)
• RT (Retrieve active PNR)
• IG (Ignore transaction)

Queue Management:
• QS (Queue Summary — view all queues)
• QT1, QT2, QT3, QT8 (Enter Queue)
• QN (Queue Next — keep & next PNR)
• QR (Queue Remove — processed & remove)
• QD (Queue Delay — delay PNR)
• QI (Queue Ignore — exit queue mode)
• QE 1 (Place active PNR in Queue 1)
